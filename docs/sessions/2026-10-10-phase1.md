## Decisions taken with the author

- Link-wise boundaries everywhere; velocity inlets use velocity bounce-back instead of Zou-He (ADR-014).
- f32 only, shifted populations; `precision: "f64"` is a warning (ADR-015).
- Gravity stays a scene property (`units::STANDARD_GRAVITY` as default); the solver applies only
  Boussinesq buoyancy and the hydrostatic pressure is added to the output (ADR-016).
- One `Solver` trait implemented by each backend, no separate `Backend` trait (ADR-017).
- The physics validation command is `cfd-cli verify` (`validate` checks scene files).

## Found along the way

- Velocity bounce-back with the *local* density makes inlets unstable with open outlets (mass grew
  ×1.5 every 5000 steps); `ρ₀ = 1` imposes the mass flux and is well posed.
- A zero-gradient outlet copied along a diagonal link picks up the shear of the profile; it now copies
  the interior neighbour along the outlet normal (flags bits 5–7). With walls it still cannot carry a
  pressure gradient → new warning `domain.noPressureReference` when there is no pressure outlet.
- Relaxing the rest populations directly left an f32 rounding bias (heat drift ~2e-8 per step);
  computing them as the remainder of the sum conserves mass and heat by construction (~40× less drift).
